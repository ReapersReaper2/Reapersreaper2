## CHAPTER 9 — "Forgehold"

### Scene 1: Ashen Peak approach

*[Ash wind. A black slope under a bruised red sky. The volcano breathes on the horizon. Mollosar climbs, Form 3, scythe over his shoulder. Luna drifts beside him, dimmer than usual — the air here tastes of iron.]*

**LUNA:** They say the forge never goes cold.

**MOLLOSAR:** Everything goes cold eventually.

**LUNA:** That's the spirit. Inspiring. Truly.

*[A shape moves below — a caravan of ash-caked porters hauling ore carts up the switchback. One of them stares at Mollosar a beat too long, then looks away fast.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** We're expected.

**LUNA:** We're *noticed*. Different thing.

**MOLLOSAR:** Here? Same thing.

*[TRIGGER: Ashen Peak region unlock. Random encounters: ash wraiths.]*

### Scene 2: The forge door

*[Forgehold squats against the mountain like a held breath — black stone, a chimney coughing orange light. The door is iron banded with something older. Mollosar knocks. Silence. Knocks again. The door opens on a wall of heat and a man built like the anvil he stands behind. This is the BLACKSMITH. His eyes go to Mollosar's cracked pauldron — the pressure fractures from Blossom, webbed fine as frost. His face closes.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** Take that armor and get out.

**MOLLOSAR:** I need it repaired.

**BLACKSMITH:** Then you need a different smith. *[Starts to close the door.]*

**LUNA:** *[bright, fast]* He'll pay double! Whatever the rate is — double! Triple if you —

**BLACKSMITH:** I don't want your credits. *[The door stops, half-closed. A beat. Quieter:]* I know what that armor's becoming. I can smell the green under the steel. Take it and get out.

*[The door shuts. Heat. Ash. Silence.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** ...He's afraid of it.

**LUNA:** He's afraid of *you*.

**MOLLOSAR:** No. He looked at the armor, not me. *[A pause.]* There's a difference, and I need to know what it is.

*[TRIGGER: Quest "The Cold Forge" begins. Objective: find another way in.]*

### Scene 3: The missing apprentice

*[Forgehold's lower yard. A boy-sized shape sits on a coal sack, knees up — PELL (assistant-created), the blacksmith's apprentice's younger brother, maybe fourteen, ash to the eyebrows. He is trying not to cry and failing at it.]*

**PELL:** You're the reaper.

**MOLLOSAR:** I am.

**PELL:** Good. Maybe you'll go where nobody else will. *[Wipes his face with a black hand.]* My brother's in the old mine. Three days. The master won't go after him. He just — he locked the forge and he won't *talk* —

**LUNA:** What's in the old mine?

**PELL:** Used to be just played-out shafts. Then something came up through the deep cut. The foreman said it looked like — like roots. Pale ones. Big as *ship masts*. They took Joren (assistant-created) — that's my brother — they took him when the east shaft caved and the master *won't go* —

**MOLLOSAR:** Why won't he go?

**PELL:** *[Small.]* Because the last time he made something for someone who went into the dark, that someone came back wrong. That's what he said. Once. Drunk.

*[Mollosar looks at the mountain. Then at the boy.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** Show me the mine entrance.

*[TRIGGER: Dungeon "The Abandoned Mine" unlocks. Party: Mollosar + Luna.]*

### Scene 4: The mine — pale roots

*[The mine. Lantern light gutters against dark older than the tunnels. The timbers groan. And through the collapsed east shaft: roots. Enormous, pale, faintly pulsing — breaking up through the shaft floor like the mountain is growing veins. They do not react to lantern light. They do not react to anything. That is worse.]*

**LUNA:** *[very quiet]* Mollosar. Those aren't tree roots.

**MOLLOSAR:** I know.

**LUNA:** What *are* they?

**MOLLOSAR:** *[A long look.]* Buried.

*[A sound — weak, human — from a pocket in the collapse. JOREN (assistant-created), the apprentice, pinned under a timber, one leg folded wrong, alive. A pale root has curled *around* the timber above him — not touching him. Holding the ceiling up.]*

**JOREN:** Don't — don't cut it — it's the only thing keeping the —

**MOLLOSAR:** I can see that.

*[Mollosar sets his scythe down. Hefts the timber with his shoulder. Bone creaks. The root pulses, once, slow — and holds. Joren drags himself clear.]*

**JOREN:** It *caught* the ceiling. When the shaft came down, it — I thought it was going to crush me, and it just — held.

**LUNA:** That's not a rescue, Mollosar. That's *farming*.

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Looking at the root. It pulses. Patient. Indifferent.]* Then we're the crop. *[Picks up his scythe.]* Can you walk?

**JOREN:** If you don't watch me do it.

*[TRIGGER: Escort sequence. The roots never attack. They never acknowledge you. [WIRING: Ancient One foreshadow flag 1/3 — log "pale roots, mine" silently.]]*

### Scene 5: The realization

*[Forgehold. The blacksmith takes Joren's weight at the door without a word — and then sees Mollosar standing in the ash behind them, and stops. Something crosses his face that isn't anger.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** You went down there.

**MOLLOSAR:** Your apprentice is alive. The roots held the shaft for him.

**BLACKSMITH:** *[Staring.]* Nobody goes down there.

**MOLLOSAR:** I know.

*[A long silence. The blacksmith looks down at the cracked pauldron Mollosar carries under his arm — he'd taken it off to lift the timber. The smith holds out his hand, almost against his will. Mollosar gives it to him. The blacksmith turns it over. His thumb finds the pressure fractures. His face changes.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** I can repair steel. Whatever you brought me isn't steel anymore.

**MOLLOSAR:** Then what is it?

**BLACKSMITH:** *[Doesn't answer. Turns the pauldron again. Quietly, to himself:]* It's *growing* through the cracks. *[Looks up.]* Who did this to you?

**MOLLOSAR:** A plant.

**BLACKSMITH:** *[A short, humorless sound.]* Of course it was a plant.

*[TRIGGER: Quest "The Cold Forge" complete. The forge door opens.]*

### Scene 6: The longsword

*[The forge, night. The blacksmith works Mollosar's mortal effects for scrap — buckles, fittings, a dented gorget. Then he lifts the old longsword. The one from the prologue. The one Mollosar carried at Bellhollow. The blacksmith goes still. He holds it like it weighs more than steel.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** ...Where did you get this.

*[Not a question. Mollosar answers it anyway.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** It was my father's.

*[Silence. The forge breathes. The blacksmith runs one thumb along the fuller, slow, like reading.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** Mallomar owed me money.

*[He goes back to work. But his hands are gentler after that — on the sword, on the pauldron, on everything. He never charges Mollosar. Not once. Not ever. Don't explain further.]*

**LUNA:** *[Later, outside, low.]* He knew your father.

**MOLLOSAR:** He knew *of* him. There's a difference.

**LUNA:** Is there? He just reforged your whole life for free.

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Looking at the sword, now cleaned, resting across his pack.]* ...Debts are remembered. Apparently even his.

*[TRIGGER: Form 4 unlock — BLACKSMITH ARMOR. Sleeker, stronger. Narration: "The last form that is entirely you. Remember this."]*

## CHAPTER 10 — "What It Decides"

### Scene 1: The metal chamber

*[Grimley's Reserve. The organic door — layered leaves, breathing faintly. Mollosar stands before it in the blacksmith's armor, Form 4, the last form that is entirely his. Grimley beside him. Tucker a few paces back, uncharacteristically without a cigar lit.]*

**GRIMLEY:** You don't have to do this.

**MOLLOSAR:** The armor's failing. You said so. The mandrake said so. The blacksmith said —

**GRIMLEY:** I said you don't *have* to. Not that you shouldn't. There's a difference, and it's the only one that matters.

**MOLLOSAR:** What will it feel like?

**GRIMLEY:** *[A pause.]* I don't know. No one's ever asked a plant to do *this* before. That's rather the point of the whole exercise.

*[Dry. Not comforting. Mollosar almost smiles.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** Your bedside manner needs work.

**GRIMLEY:** I'm a gardener, not a physician. Gardeners bury things.

**TUCKER:** *[Quiet.]* For what it's worth... I'd rather walk beside whatever comes out of there than carry what goes in.

*[Mollosar looks at him. Nods once. Steps through the door. It seals behind him like a mouth closing.]*

*[TRIGGER: Fade to black. Time passes — the game does not show it. [WIRING: Form 5 unlock — PLANT ARMOR I.]]*

### Scene 2: The arm with opinions

*[Morning. The nursery's side room. Mollosar wakes on a stone slab, a mandrake's leaf-blanket tucked around him with absurd tenderness. His right arm — is not his right arm. Green-black plating, vine-muscle, small leaves at the joints that open and close with his breath. He stares at it.]*

*[He raises it. It rises. He turns the hand over. The hand turns over.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Soft.]* ...Hello.

*[The leaves at the wrist flutter. Once.]*

*[He sits up. Swings his legs off the slab. Stands. The arm hangs at his side, and — without any instruction, without any thought — the fingers curl, slowly, deliberately, into a fist. Then open again.]*

*[Mollosar goes very still.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** I didn't do that.

*[The arm does nothing. The leaves at the wrist are very still. Too still.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Quieter.]* Did you?

*[Nothing. A leaf trembles — or the air moved. He can't tell. That's the worst part: he can't tell.]*

*[TRIGGER: Form 5 active. New ability: arm abilities (contextual). [WIRING: Bloom meter introduced silently — no UI yet.]]*

### Scene 3: Tendrils (Form 6)

*[A week later. The garden at dusk. Mollosar trains alone — or tries to. The tendrils came three days ago, unspooling from his shoulder and spine while he slept, and now they move when he moves, mostly. He throws a practice cut. A tendril lashes out a half-second *before* the scythe, correcting his angle. The cut lands perfectly.]*

*[Mollosar lowers the scythe. Looks at the tendril. It coils back against his shoulder, innocent as a sleeping cat.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** That was mine.

*[The tendril does not respond. It has never responded. It doesn't need to.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** Wasn't it.

*[TRIGGER: Form 6 unlock — PLANT ARMOR II. Tendril traversal (grapple) enabled.]*

### Scene 4: Luna

*[Night. The nursery's clay pots, moonlight through the canopy. The little Reaper's Reaper seedling sits unimpressive in its pot. Luna hovers near it — lingering a beat too long, the way she does. Mollosar watches her from the doorway, his plant arm cradled against his chest like something that might bite.]*

**LUNA:** You're staring.

**MOLLOSAR:** You're hovering.

**LUNA:** I'm a fairy. It's my whole thing.

*[She drifts over. Settles near his shoulder — not on the plant side. She's never once landed on the plant side.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** Does it bother you? The arm.

**LUNA:** Should it?

**MOLLOSAR:** It's not *me*, Luna.

**LUNA:** *[A pause. Then, firmly:]* You held a bridge at Bellhollow with a sword and a shield and no powers at all. You carried a boy out of a mine yesterday with a shoulder that isn't yours. The arm is new. You're not. Stop auditing yourself.

**MOLLOSAR:** ...When did you get wise?

**LUNA:** I've always been wise. You were just taller before, so it was less noticeable.

*[The smallest smile. The leaves at his wrist flutter — once — and for the first time it doesn't feel wrong. Just... new.]*

### Scene 5: The kindness (Bloom tutorial)

*[The garden, midday. A mandrake child — small, root-limbed, all eyes — chases a drifting seed-pod beneath the drying racks. Above: a lantern, heavy brass, its hook rusted through. It gives way.]*

*[It falls.]*

*[Mollosar is twenty feet away, back turned, arguing with Tucker about cigar blends. He does not see it. He does not move.]*

*[His arm does.]*

*[A tendril unspools — fast, silent, precise — and catches the lantern six inches above the child's head. Holds it. Gently. The child stares up. The tendril lowers the lantern to the moss like setting down a sleeping thing. Then coils back to Mollosar's shoulder.]*

*[Everyone stares. Tucker. The mandrakes. The child. Mollosar stares — at his own arm.]*

**GRIMLEY:** *[Very quietly.]* Did you tell it to do that?

**MOLLOSAR:** ...No.

*[Beat. The mandrake child walks over and hugs the tendril. The tendril goes rigid with what can only be described as panic.]*

**TUCKER:** *[Lighting his cigar, deeply moved and covering it badly.]* Well. That's the most useful any of us has been all week, and it wasn't even *trying*.

*[TRIGGER: Bloom meter tutorial complete — taught as KINDNESS. [WIRING: Bloom UI unlocks; decays at Lantern Shrines and over time.]]*

## SIDE QUEST 1 — "Tucker and the Sloths"

*[Full questline. Give it the budget of a chapter.]*

### Scene 1: Departure

*[Dawn. The garden's edge. Tucker's satchel is packed — bedroll, dried mushrooms, his katana, three cigars in a tin he checks twice. Mollosar finds him at the treeline.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** You're leaving.

**TUCKER:** For a while.

**MOLLOSAR:** Where?

**TUCKER:** Down. *[A pause.]* My kind — the old kind, the ground kind — they den in the Warrens. If what's coming is as bad as Grimley's face says it is... I should ask them to stand with us.

**MOLLOSAR:** "Very dangerous," he said. Your words.

**TUCKER:** My words, yes.

**MOLLOSAR:** I'm coming with you.

**TUCKER:** No.

**MOLLOSAR:** Tucker —

**TUCKER:** *[Gentle. Final.]* This is a sloth asking sloths. A reaper walking in with me makes it a *delegation*. It has to be just me. *[Shoulders the satchel.]* A slower world still gets there, Mollosar.

*[He walks into the treeline. Doesn't look back — sloths never do; it's a neck thing.]*

**LUNA:** You're going to follow him.

**MOLLOSAR:** Obviously.

**LUNA:** He knew you would.

**MOLLOSAR:** Obviously.

*[TRIGGER: Quest "Tucker and the Sloths" begins. Objective: follow at a distance.]*

### Scene 2: The Subterranean Warrens

*[Down. Past the root cellar, past the old growth, through a fissure in the garden's floor that Grimley pretends not to know about. The Subterranean Warrens: collapsed temples with gods worn faceless, fossilized forests like black coral, burial grounds with stones older than the afterlife's maps.]*

*[And through the ceilings — roots. Grimley's forest, piercing down from above. The surface is linked to the underground. The world is connected; the game should feel it.]*

*[Tucker moves through it all with a quiet reverence, touching nothing. Mollosar and Luna trail a half-hour behind, quiet as they can be, which isn't very.]*

**LUNA:** *[Whispering.]* He's *praying*. I've never seen him pray.

**MOLLOSAR:** He's not praying. He's remembering. There's a difference.

*[Deeper. The lowest warren. The air changes — thicker, warmer, wrong in a way that has nothing to do with temperature.]*

*[A seam in the rock. And through it: roots. Enormous. PALE. Bigger than the mine's — some as thick as temple columns — pulsing faintly, slowly, like something asleep that dreams in blood-pressure.]*

*[Tucker stops. He does not go near it. His hand finds his katana and does not draw it.]*

**TUCKER:** *[Very low, to himself.]* ...Not mine to wake.

*[He gives the seam a wide berth — the widest of the whole journey — and walks on. Neither should you.]*

*[TRIGGER: [WIRING: Ancient One foreshadow flag 2/3 — log "pale roots, Warrens" silently. No combat here. The roots never acknowledge you.]]*

### Scene 3: The Ground Sloths

*[The warren opens into a cavern so large the far wall is weather. And they are there.]*

*[The Ground Sloths. Massive — wider than tall, built like moving stone. Fur matted and earth-stained: ash gray, deep umber, dull olive, rusted brown. Claws mineral-encrusted, thick as beams — they tunneled through stone for centuries. Small, deep-set, reflective eyes. Ancient, not savage. They feel like buried gods.]*

*[Tucker walks forward alone. Mollosar hangs back in the dark with Luna, watching.]*

*[One of the great shapes stirs. A voice like grinding millstones:]*

**SLOTH ELDER (assistant-created):** Another small one. Come to stare?

**TUCKER:** I've come to ask.

*[A sound — amusement, or rockfall. Hard to tell.]*

**SLOTH ELDER (assistant-created):** The earth does not move for ants.

**TUCKER:** I'm not asking the earth to move. I'm asking my *kin* to stand.

**SLOTH ELDER (assistant-created):** Kin. *[The great head turns, eyes catching the light like wet coal.]* You smell of gardens. Of *tended* things. What does a tended sloth know of the deep?

**TUCKER:** That it's worth protecting. Even from up here.

*[Silence. The kind with weight. Then — movement, from the largest shape, the one that hasn't moved at all. GRAVEMAW rises. The cavern seems to tilt.]*

### Scene 4: Gravemaw

*[No trial. No test. Gravemaw looks at Tucker for a long time — and recognizes him the way stone recognizes rain. Instinctual. Immediate.]*

**GRAVEMAW:** A sloth does not abandon another sloth.

*[The elder stirs, displeased.]*

**SLOTH ELDER (assistant-created):** He is *garden*-raised. He has forgotten —

**GRAVEMAW:** He remembers enough to come *down*. That is the whole of it.

*[To Tucker, simply:]*

**GRAVEMAW:** Why have you come, little one?

**TUCKER:** *[And here it is — the courage to ask for help, which is the whole of Tucker:]* The world above is going to break. I have friends up there. *People* — worth protecting. I can't hold it alone. I won't insult you by pretending I could.

*[A long silence. Then Gravemaw settles — the mountain deciding to be a mountain.]*

**GRAVEMAW:** Speak, then. Tell us of these people.

### Scene 5: Campfire

*[Night in the Warrens. A campfire — sloth-sized, which is to say enormous — and Tucker telling stories badly and wonderfully: the garden, the mandrakes, the reaper who carried a boy out of a mine. The great shapes listen. One of them, a giant female with rust-brown fur gone silver at the shoulders, watches Tucker with an attention that is not about the story.]*

*[A sound from the dark — wrong, fast. A subterranean predator, all teeth and hunger, launches at the fire's edge, at Tucker —]*

*[— and stops. Because Gravemaw's claw has come down between them, silent as snowfall, and the predator is simply... elsewhere. Fled. The leader never looked up.]*

**GRAVEMAW:** *[Without looking up.]* That was brave.

**TUCKER:** *[Quietly.]* ...Sitting here?

**GRAVEMAW:** Coming down.

*[Later. The fire low. The female sloth settles near Tucker — near, the way mountains are near.]*

**FEMALE SLOTH:** You should not sleep alone.

**TUCKER:** *[A long pause.]* ...That's very kind.

**FEMALE SLOTH:** It is practical. You are small. The dark is large.

**TUCKER:** *[To Mollosar, later, deeply flustered in a way sloths are not built for:]* She meant it *practically*.

**LUNA:** Sure she did.

**TUCKER:** She *said* practical.

**MOLLOSAR:** Mm.

**TUCKER:** You're both terrible.

*[Play it gentle and funny. The game needs air.]*

### Scene 6: "He stays."

*[Morning. The sloths gather — a slow tide of stone and fur. Tucker stands before them, satchel packed, expecting the verdict he's been bracing for since the surface.]*

**GRAVEMAW:** You asked us to stand with your people.

**TUCKER:** I did.

**GRAVEMAW:** The deep does not march to the surface for *causes*. *[A pause. The great eyes find him.]* But a sloth does not abandon another sloth. And you are *ours*, garden or no.

*[To the gathered:]*

**GRAVEMAW:** He stays.

*[Not "he may stay." Not "he is welcome." **He stays.** Protection is instinctual, not earned — the deliberate mirror-inverse of the Soul Council, which tests, judges, manipulates.]*

**TUCKER:** *[Voice thick.]* ...I don't know what to —

**GRAVEMAW:** Then don't. Oaths are remembered. Debts are remembered. Go back up, little one. When the ground shakes, *listen*.

### Scene 7: The walk back

*[The fissure, climbing toward the light. Tucker is quiet for a long time. Then:]*

**TUCKER:** Raw power isn't enough. Even monsters need allies.

**MOLLOSAR:** Are you calling yourself a monster?

**TUCKER:** I'm calling *them* monsters, and they still said yes. That's rather the point.

*[A beat. Then, lighter:]*

**TUCKER:** She really did mean it practically, you know.

**LUNA:** Mm-hm.

**TUCKER:** Sloths don't *do* —

**MOLLOSAR:** Tucker.

**TUCKER:** ...Yes?

**MOLLOSAR:** Smoke your cigar.

*[TRIGGER: Quest complete. [WIRING: Set flag "sloth_oath" — changes the final act. Tucker rejoins the party.]]*

## CHAPTER 11 — "The Broken Mini Cannon"

*[Side Quest 2, mainline-adjacent. Canon gives one beat — a broken revolver absorbed while Mollosar sleeps. A game needs a quest. Here's the quest.]*

### Scene 1: The cache

*[Shika territory, after the bounty. A hollow cairn off the trail — old, unmarked, wrong in a way that suggests someone wanted it found by the right person and missed by everyone else. Inside, wrapped in oilcloth gone to rot: a revolver.]*

*[Ancient. Mechanically frozen — the cylinder won't turn, the hammer won't cock. Spiritually inert — Luna holds her light to it and it gives back nothing. Small. Almost toy-like.]*

**LUNA:** It's dead.

**MOLLOSAR:** It's *broken*. There's a difference.

**LUNA:** Is there? It's a paperweight with ambitions.

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Turning it over.]* It's old. Older than the forge, I'd guess. Someone carried this a long time.

**LUNA:** Someone with small hands and poor judgment.

**MOLLOSAR:** We'll sell it. Or melt it down. The blacksmith can get scrap value.

**LUNA:** *[Quietly.]* ...it feels… waiting.

**MOLLOSAR:** It's a gun, Luna. It doesn't feel anything.

**LUNA:** Then why are you still holding it?

*[Mollosar looks down. He's been turning it over for a full minute. He puts it in his pack a little too carefully.]*

*[TRIGGER: Key item acquired — "Ancient Revolver" (broken). Flavor: "Mechanically frozen. Spiritually inert. Small. Almost toy-like."]*

### Scene 2: No buyers

*[Forgehold. Mollosar lays the revolver on the blacksmith's counter. The smith picks it up, weighs it, sets it down.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** I'll melt it. Scrap value.

**MOLLOSAR:** That's the idea.

**BLACKSMITH:** *[Already turning to the furnace.]* Leave it.

*[Mollosar leaves it. He gets as far as the door —]*

**LUNA:** *[In his head, or hovering at his ear — urgent, uncharacteristically so:]* Don't.

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Stops.]* ...It's scrap, Luna.

**LUNA:** It feels… waiting.

**MOLLOSAR:** You said that.

**LUNA:** I'm saying it again because you didn't listen the first time.

> **Choice:** (A) Leave it for scrap. / (B) Take it back.

*[If (A): Mollosar leaves. The blacksmith nods, tosses it toward the scrap bin — and misses. It lands upright, barrel down, balanced on its grip. Both of them stare at it. The blacksmith picks it up, sets it down flat, deliberately. Turns away. When he looks back — it's upright again.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** *[A long stare.]* ...Take it with you.

**MOLLOSAR:** I thought you wanted the scrap.

**BLACKSMITH:** I want a *quiet* forge.

*[If (B): Mollosar takes it back. The blacksmith shrugs.]*

**BLACKSMITH:** Your funeral. Or someone's.

*[Either way: the revolver stays with Mollosar. That night, if the player tries to sell it — to Murray, to a fence in the hub, to anyone — no buyer wants it. One fence won't touch it. One offers half price, then takes it back mid-handshake, pale. That's the joke and the tell.]*

*[TRIGGER: [WIRING: Choice flag "revolver_sold" — if sold, it is back in the pack the next morning. No explanation. Do not explain until the absorption.]]*

### Scene 3: The absorption

*[The night before Mount Dragoni. Camp on the mountain's shoulder. The fire burned low hours ago. Mollosar sleeps — truly sleeps, the deep-down sleep of someone who has decided tomorrow is going to be bad and there's no use rehearsing it.]*

*[His plant arm does not sleep.]*

*[It rises — slowly, silently, with a care that is almost tender — and reaches into his pack. Finds the revolver. Lifts it.]*

*[The leaves at the wrist open. The vines part. The revolver sinks into the arm the way a stone sinks into dark water — no splash, no struggle. The plating closes over it. Nothing is left behind. Not dust. Not oil. Nothing.]*

*[The arm settles back at his side. The leaves close. In the morning, Mollosar pats his pack for the revolver out of habit —]*

*[— and stops.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** ...Where —

*[He empties the pack. Everything accounted for. Everything except.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** *[To his arm, flatly:]* You took it.

*[The arm does nothing. A leaf trembles.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** That was *mine*. That was — it was scrap, it was *nothing*, and you took it while I was *asleep* —

*[He stops. Breathes. Looks at the arm for a long moment.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Quietly, annoyed — not devastated. Annoyed.]* ...Fine. But we're discussing boundaries.

*[The emotional mismatch is important: he is irritated, not horrified. The plant didn't steal power. It stole DESIGN — structure, intent, cyclical logic, contained violence. A blueprint, not a weapon.]*

**LUNA:** *[Hovering, fascinated despite herself:]* It waited until you were asleep. That's *polite*, for it.

**MOLLOSAR:** Don't defend it.

**LUNA:** I'm not defending it. I'm *studying* it. There's a difference, and it's the only one that matters.

*[TRIGGER: Form 7 unlock — CANNON ASSIMILATION. The right arm is now a living cannon chamber. [WIRING: Arm cannon as limited ability — high cost, high recoil. Not technology. Biology that learned an idea.]]*

### Scene 4: The seed

*[Later. The mountain path. Mollosar flexes the arm — the chamber is there now, under the plating, a hollow he can feel when he breathes. He hasn't fired it. He doesn't want to know yet.]*

**MOLLOSAR:** The blast wasn't the point.

**LUNA:** What blast? You haven't —

**MOLLOSAR:** *[Not hearing her, or hearing something else.]* If it ever fires — the force. The *force* was the point. *[He looks at his fist. Then, slowly, at his calves. His heels.]* ...Propulsion.

**LUNA:** Mollosar. You're scaring me a little.

**MOLLOSAR:** Good. I'm scaring me too.

*[Plant it here. Pay it off in Act 3.]*

## THE ASTRAL VILLAGE

### Scene 1: Arrival
