## What the current game actually does

There are **two combat paths**, not one uniformly turn-based game:

1. **Field combat:** `player.gd` accepts movement/weapon input; `scythe.gd` advances attacks in physics time and calls `take_hit` on overlapping `hittable` bodies. `invader.gd` pursues its target, telegraphs and lands melee damage. Player health is `vigor` in GameState. This path already supports direct control of Mollosar.
2. **Creature battles:** `wild_zone.gd` selects a creature and calls BattleManager. Wild encounters keep the room loaded but `seamless_battle.gd` locks Mollosar's movement/input and suspends his touch controls. It embeds `battle.gd`, which constructs one player creature and one enemy creature and waits for FIGHT/CATCH/SWITCH/ITEM/FLEE commands. Boss/trainer launches use `battle.tscn` and the same turn loop.

The seam is structural: a seamless room does **not** make its embedded turn battle real-time. Retain the useful field mechanics and encounter lifecycle, then replace the creature battle's two-unit command model with a shared fighter/companion runtime.

## Reuse without preserving the obsolete battle model

- `player.gd`, `scythe.gd`, `invader.gd`: direct movement, charge, active windows, hit deduplication, knockback and combat feedback are a useful starting point. Current locks/animation timing remain behavior to test, not carte blanche to rewrite canon.
- `combat_time.gd` provides scoped hitstop. `encounter_time.gd` provides opt-in encounter scaling and cancellation. Coordinate them through one combat update path; do not introduce `Engine.time_scale`, double-scaled timers, or independent clocks for each unit. Existing player movement uses unscaled delta, so the exact slow-motion boundary must be explicit.
- `BattleManager` retains orchestration, duplicate-start prevention, return metadata, `clear_pending()`, Twitch gating and cooldown stamping. Its participant/config validation must change even though the orchestration survives.
- Party identities (`instance_ref`), storage, health/XP helpers and save formats survive. Use stable party identity for active companions rather than only a mutable array index, particularly if party order changes.
- Keep `CreatureAI` temperament/ability selection, traits, damage arithmetic and level growth where applicable. Add spatial targeting/action scheduling around them; do not call the brain every frame.
- Peaceful `encounter_session.gd` and Reserve scythe reactions stay separate: notice/offer/tend/withdraw/guide/scare are not automatic combat, damage or capture. Existing Reserve motion is presentation only.
- `wrath_ally.gd` is a future endgame record with `turn_order: after_player`, not a live follower controller. Its turn assumption also needs migration when that encounter is implemented; it does not solve companions today.

## Smallest proposed rework sequence (stage 2 only)

These are bounded slices for review, **not code delivered by this note**.

1. **Adopt the contract and close the blocking design seams.** Log the folded spec revision and written stage-2 scope. Resolve fighter stats/damage bridging and affected encounter ownership. Separate `main_fight` from `creature_duel`; define participant identities/side/role and optional companion lists. Update manager producers and `battle.gd`'s whitelist together. Solo fights must validate with no party creature. Preserve existing pending lifecycle and save compatibility.
2. **Make one fighter-only fight real-time.** Use Mollosar and an existing field enemy actor in the same room. Bridge field contact to the adopted damage model; make the battle director own registration/resolution, not input. Retain touch movement/attack, scoped timing and pause/dialogue ownership. Exercise victory, Mollosar defeat, allowed flee-channel interruption and forced teardown. This verifies the foundation without inventing companion content.
3. **Add one optional autonomous player companion.** Bind an existing owned creature by instance identity; spawn a world actor that follows, acquires hostile targets, and attacks with its own cooldown/phase state. Reuse `CreatureAI` once per action opportunity. Companion zero HP removes only that actor and excludes it for the rest of the fight. Sync it once at resolution; no duplicate reward/defeat events. Add explicit target focus and keep the no-companion path valid.
4. **Prove targetable enemy companions.** Add one authored fighter-plus-creature test encounter. Target either independently; enemy creature damage can reach Mollosar and player companions through the same adapter. Keep the encounter active until its required enemy combatants are out. A fighter-only encounter must not spawn a creature. This is required for full §1 coverage even though Hush labels enemy companions later than the first MVP.
5. **Migrate actual callers and retire turns from main progression.** Convert approved NPC/story encounters and wild/hunt routing with their metadata intact. Replace the modal command HUD; update dependent tests with each migrated path. Keep unresolved cutting/capture work out of the MVP. Creature duels require an explicit opt-in side-mode entry, CPU or direct creature control, and the same real-time engine; never retain `_run_turn` as a shipping side-mode shortcut.

Use actor composition for presentation from the first slice. Do not attach unapproved Bellmaw/pitcher art or expand armor_plant while waiting for records. No new creature art was generated or admitted here. The Form 1 complete-APPROVED-manifest loader remains untouched; five available strips are not enough to enable it.
